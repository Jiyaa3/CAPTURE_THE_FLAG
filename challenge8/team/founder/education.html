Education

Part 2 of 6

Marsh earned a B.Sc. in Computer Science from Reed College in 1984, where her senior thesis modeled fault tolerance in early packet-switched networks. She went on to a Ph.D. in Distributed Systems at the University of Washington, completed in 1990, advised by a professor she later described as "allergic to consensus algorithms that couldn't be explained on a napkin."
