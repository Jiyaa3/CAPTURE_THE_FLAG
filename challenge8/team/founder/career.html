Career

Part 3 of 6

After her doctorate, Marsh spent six years at Bell Labs working on replication protocols before leaving in 1996 to consult independently. In 1998 she co-founded RootJ Technologies out of a shared office space in Portland, initially building custom message queues for logistics clients.

She served as CEO for over two decades, stepping back from daily operations in 2019 while remaining involved as an advisor.
