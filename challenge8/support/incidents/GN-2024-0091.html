Incident GN-2024-0091

Filed January 2024 · Status: Resolved · Severity: SEV-2

Summary: An internal audit identified a legacy FTP service (see platform status) that had been left in place after a 2019 migration and was decommissioned as part of this incident's remediation.

Impact: No customer-facing systems were affected. The service in question was used only for internal file transfer between two legacy build machines.

Root cause: The FTP service was missed during the 2019 decommissioning sweep and remained reachable from the internal network for longer than intended.

Remediation:

  • Legacy FTP service decommissioned January 19, 2024.
  • Internal network scan performed to confirm no other legacy services remained.
  • Credential rotation performed for all accounts with access to the affected host.

Timeline: Detected 2024-01-14, contained 2024-01-15, remediated 2024-01-19, postmortem published 2024-01-26.

This report has been reviewed and redacted for public posting in line with our transparency policy.
