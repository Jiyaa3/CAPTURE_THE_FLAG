Culture

RootJ has stayed under 90 employees by design. We write things down, default to async communication across our distributed team, and hold a quarterly all-hands that Elena Marsh insisted on keeping to 45 minutes, a tradition the company still keeps.

"If a meeting can't fit in 45 minutes, it should have been a document." — attributed to Elena Marsh, internal wiki, undated
