Later years and legacy

Part 6 of 6

Marsh was diagnosed with a long-term illness in 2018 and gradually reduced her role at RootJ, formally stepping down as CEO in 2019 while staying on the board. She died on January 14, 2021, at age 58. The company's quarterly all-hands — a tradition she started and kept to 45 minutes — continues under current leadership.
