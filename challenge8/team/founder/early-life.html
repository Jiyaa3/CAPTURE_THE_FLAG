Early life

Part 1 of 6

Elena Marsh was born on March 3, 1962, in Portland, Oregon, the second of three children. Her father repaired industrial refrigeration units; her mother taught middle school math. Marsh has described a childhood spent taking apart the family's rotary phone and reassembling it "close enough that nobody noticed for a week."

She attended Lincoln High School, where she ran the school's first computer club on a single donated terminal, and graduated in 1980.
