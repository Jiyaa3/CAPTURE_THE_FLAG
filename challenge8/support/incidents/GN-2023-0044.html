Incident GN-2023-0044

Filed September 2023 · Status: Resolved

Summary: A misconfigured retry policy in the RootAPI gateway caused a burst of duplicate webhook deliveries to two partner integrations over a 40-minute window.

Impact: No data loss. Affected partners were notified and duplicate events were deduplicated on replay.

Root cause: A deployment removed an idempotency key check that had not been covered by integration tests.

Follow-up: Idempotency checks are now part of the standard pre-deploy checklist.
