Our history

RootJ began in 1998 as a two-person consulting practice above a bicycle repair shop in Portland, Oregon. The founders, Dr. Elena Marsh and a small rotating cast of contract engineers, spent the first three years building custom message queues for logistics companies before releasing an early version of what would become RootOS in 2003.

The company survived the 2001 downturn on consulting revenue alone, took its first outside investment in 2006, and shipped RootAPI in 2011 after acquiring a small API tooling startup out of Seattle.
